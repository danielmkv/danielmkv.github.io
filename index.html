Research

I build AI research tools and study how people experience them. At UW, my work connects human–AI interaction, reproducible research workflows, and hands-on evaluation.

I’m interested in both sides of that relationship: how AI can help people ask questions and explore ideas, and how studying people’s experiences can help us build better AI systems.

Empathetic Help-Seeking System

Human/AI Interaction · UW · April 2026–Present

Can changing the wording of a response affect someone’s willingness to ask for help? I built an LLM-assisted response-rewriting prototype and compared 15+ strategies across three models.

I conducted and analyzed a 20-participant study measuring perceived empathy and willingness to seek help. The project connects AI prototyping with human-centered evaluation: building a working system, then studying how people actually experience it.

The Harrowing Tale of Human Trafficking

Data Visualization · CSE 442 · Winter 2026

Paired a scroll-driven 3D globe with linked demographic charts, country-level exploration, and age and gender filters, combining guided storytelling with open-ended analysis while making reporting biases and data limitations explicit.
