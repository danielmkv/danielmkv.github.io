Research

I’m interested in human–AI interaction: how people use, understand, and work with AI systems. How can we make these systems more helpful? What makes working with them feel intuitive, and where do things get confusing?

I’m also interested in the opposite question; how humans can help improve AI models, whether that’s through RLHF or other methods! There’s a lot we can learn in both directions :)

Selected projects

contraiλ

AWS Hacks · May 2026

Built an event-driven forecasting pipeline across five AWS services in under 24 hours, using real-time NOAA GFS weather data to predict contrail-favorable airspace. Atmospheric scoring produces roughly 1,000 polygons across five intensity tiers every six hours, with around 20-second inference and operating costs below $1 per day.

Empathetic Help-Seeking System

Human/AI Interaction · April 2026–Present

Building an LLM-powered prototype that rewrites peer responses to reduce shame in online help-seeking, testing 15+ strategies across three models. Designed a 20-participant study to measure perceived empathy and willingness to seek help, with consent and recruitment procedures grounded in 10+ HCI papers.

The Harrowing Tale of Human Trafficking

Data Visualization · CSE 442 · Winter 2026

Co-created an interactive exploration of reported human trafficking across five global regions using CTDC’s privacy-preserving synthetic dataset. Paired a scroll-driven 3D globe with linked demographic charts, country-level exploration, and age and gender filters, combining guided storytelling with open-ended analysis while making reporting biases and data limitations explicit.

Earned a perfect score, a rare distinction in CSE 442.
